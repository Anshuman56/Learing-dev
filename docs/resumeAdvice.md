## What a recruiter actually does:

spends about 10 seconds scanning your resume. Their software also reads the text, so simple layout and correct words matter.

## The bullet formula:

Action verb (past tense) + what you built + how (tech) + why or for whom

## Wrong:

"You can search any city and get the weather." That's a user manual; it describes the app, not your work.

## Right shape, on a made-up project (don't copy it):

"Built a bus timing app for small towns using Next.js and MongoDB, so passengers can check times without calling the depot."

## Verbs to start bullets with:

Built, Designed, Added, Implemented, Deployed, Interviewed, Researched, Rewrote, Replaced, Fixed.

## Use real numbers you can defend in an interview. Count honestly:

how many sellers, buyers and farmers have you talked to across the haat visits? A bullet like "Interviewed [your number] sellers and buyers at village markets to test assumptions" is the kind of line recruiters remember.
