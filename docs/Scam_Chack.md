## How to spot a scam job post

You asked, so here's the list. If a post has any one of these, skip it:

- Asks you to pay anything: registration fee, training fee, laptop deposit, "security deposit", certification fee. Real employers never charge you.
- "Guaranteed job after our course." That's a course sale, not a job.
- An offer without a real interview, or an "interview" only by WhatsApp or Telegram chat.
- The recruiter uses a Gmail or Yahoo address for a big company, or the company has no website or LinkedIn page.
- Salary far too high for 0-2 years of experience, with a vague description like "work from home, earn ₹50k/month."
- Asks for Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details before a written offer letter.
- Pressure: "reply within 1 hour or lose the offer."
